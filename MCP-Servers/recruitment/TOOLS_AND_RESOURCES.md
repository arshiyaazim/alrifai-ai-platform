# Recruitment Tools and Resources

The complete implementation-ready catalog, schemas, permission matrix, error model, and examples are in [`FINAL_IMPLEMENTATION_SPEC.md`](FINAL_IMPLEMENTATION_SPEC.md).

Final tools (18): `find_applicant`, `get_applicant`, `list_recruitment_roles`, `list_open_vacancies`, `get_role_details`, `get_recruitment_policy`, `get_application_status`, `get_joining_requirements`, `register_interest`, `create_application`, `update_applicant_profile`, `record_applicant_document`, `update_application_status`, `record_interview`, `record_joining_readiness`, `approve_applicant`, `mark_joining_pending`, `convert_applicant_to_employee`.

Resources (9): policy, role catalog, role details, open vacancies, applicant profile, application, status/history, joining requirements, and scoped recruitment conversation context. Resources are service-backed views, not raw tables. Role-based applications are allowed year-round without a Vacancy; Vacancy is optional context. Conversation context includes trusted Person/current-phone/platform references, ordered message/context references, active topic, semantic extraction, confidence, evidence, and applicable Admin instruction version; raw messages remain Conversations-owned. Mutations require identity evidence, policy validation, trusted capability, idempotency, and audit. Applicant document intake exposes metadata/reference and provenance with `received`/`linked`/`reviewed`/`formally_verified` distinctions; AI may extract fields or prepare a draft, but the Recruitment service decides and Workforce owns formal employee-document verification after handoff.
Where an Employee is already trusted, resources may expose the normalized business Employee ID according to authorization. Contact-number observations cannot mutate Employee ID; explicit edits belong to the authorized Workforce/frontend workflow.
