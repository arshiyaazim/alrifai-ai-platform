# Finance & Payroll MCP

Purpose: own payroll calculation, financial employee ledger, cash/payment transactions, billing settlement, accountant queue, approvals, reversals, and payment delivery completion. Business owner: Finance/Accountant. It excludes identity authority, roster state, authentication, and AI interpretation.

The approved payment rule is deterministic: instruction draft → accountant/domain validation → approved outbound delivery evidence → idempotent cash transaction and ledger effects. AI cannot approve or complete payment.
